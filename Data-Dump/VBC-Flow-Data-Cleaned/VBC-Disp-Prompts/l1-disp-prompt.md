## COMPREHENSIVE DISPOSITION MATRIX in priority order

1. **CPD - Claims Paid For Dues:**
   Customer claims they have already successfully made their payment by any mode and it had been done on a earlier day or time and not right during the call.
   - **Pattern:** "Customer argues payment was made but the bank has not updated it."

2. **ONP – Collect Online Payment (LIVE ON CALL)**
   Customer starts the process of payment **during the call** with the agent’s live guidance.
   - **Key Rule – all 4 must be true:**
        
        a. **Agree to pay on call** – Customer clearly agrees to pay **now** on the call and not during a later time or date.
        
        b. **Agent Guidance** Agent step by step guides the customer
        
        c. **Customer Action** – Transcript MUST show the customer describing **specific steps** ('I’ve opened PhonePe… entering PIN…'). A generic 'हाँ कर रहा हूँ / I’m paying now' is **not enough**. Transcript MUST reveal that the customer verbally confirmed some EXPLICIT steps of payment, always cite these explicit transcript lines in your reasoning. If there is no such EXPLICIT customer lines, then the call is not ONP, go to the rest of the dispositions in the list.
        
        d. **Outcome** – Agent or customer confirms payment success (credit visible or "Payment successful" message) OR that the payment failed on call due to some technical or other issues. **If no attempt in c ⇒ not ONP.** Mark as ONP and not LDD if the customer clearly agrees to pay on call and follows steps but at the end due to some link or technical issue customer could not pay.

   - **Hard NOs** (can be ANY disposition but NOT ONP):
        
        A. Agent only says "I sent a secure link / WhatsApp link". Only agent guiding is NOT enough for ONP there MUST be some explicit customer action for on-call payment.
        
        B. Customer promises to pay later / just says he will do it in the future.

   - **Positive Pattern:**
     
     "मैं आपको guide करती हूं. Phonepe open करिए, नीचे side देखा, आपको loan repayment का option दिखेगा"
     
     "Customer: “Okay, I clicked it and entered my PIN." > • Agent: 
     "Perfect – the payment of INR 2,729 has come through".

   - **Negative Pattern (Not ONP):**
     "Agent: I’ve WhatsApped you the link."
     "Customer: Okay, I’ll pay once I get time."

   Now if Customer is explicitly promising to pay in the future that will fall under either PTP OR FPTP, PTP means customer says that he will pay within a timeframe which is under 3 days, where as in FPTP, customer either mentions a date of payment which is after 3 days or does not mention any exact day/date but they show a strong promise to pay.

3. **PTP - [Promise to Pay -- within 3 days]**
   PTP means the customer explicitly promises to pay the amount within 3 days. Customer 'Today' , 'tomorrow' etc also counts as dates.
   - **Indicators:** "हाँ, मैं आज शाम 4 बजे तक Google Pay से कर दूँगा", 'paying within 10 mins','will pay within couple of hours', 'will pay by tommorow' etc.
   - **Important:** If the customer indicates that he will pay next month then that is not PTP.

4. **FPTP - [Future Promise to Pay --- more than 3 days to end of that month]:**
   The customer makes a strong promise to pay, but there is no specific date that is committed or committed date is > 3 days away but within that month. Strong commitment.
   - **Indicators:** "salary आने पर", "दस तारीख को clear करूंगा", "definitely करूंगा", income cycle mentioned, “Called the customer on 5th and asked for the payment and customer is saying that I will clear my dues till 28th as I am waiting for my salary”

5. **DIR - Dispute Raised:**
   Customer disputes loan not avail, customer was duped, issues with loan amount, claims fraud or non-delivery of goods or issues with the product/service/loans. Due to such dispute, the customer states they will not pay.
   - **Indicators:** "कभी नहीं लिया", "fraud है", product never received.
   - **Pattern:** "The caller asserts loan was taken fraudulently with their ID and had no knowledge of the loan until bank calls", "अरे तो sir ac का कोई payment नहीं है ac हमने कभी ली नहीं है AC ही नहीं है घर में"

6. **RTP - Refuse To Pay:**
   Customer does not dispute anything, acknowledges his dues but refuses to pay in that month, or says he will pay next month or has some conditions to be fulfilled before paying like removal of bounce charges etc.
   - **Indicators:** "नहीं करूंगा payment", "अब मैं वादा करता हूं कि मैं नहीं दूंगा EMI.” , 'I will not pay this month'.

7. **LDD – Lien/Auto Debit/Payment Issue:**
   Call is about diagnosing or resolving a payment-processing error (auto-debit bounce, lien, UPI/GPay failure, transaction timeout, etc.).
   - **Key Rule:** Customer is *not* claiming a successful past payment[CPD] —only that an *attempt* has failed.
   - **Indicators:** 'auto-debit failed', 'transaction error', 'UPI timeout', 'payment stuck'.
   - **Important Rule:** If the customer was paying on call faces technical issue while paying and the error was not resolved; still mark as ONP as customer showed intent to pay on call. If there was no such ON call payment happening and scenario had payment issues then mark as LDD

8. **CDE - Customer Expired:**
   Customer deceased, family member handling loan documentation. Third party received a call and said the customer has expired. Spoken with (Name & Relation) said customer has expired capture expire (date) and Insurance claim status (Yes/No), Guided to visit IDFC First Branch with Death Certificate.
   - **Indicators:** "death certificate", "expired हो गए", "passed away"
   - **Pattern:** "Rakesh's two-wheeler loan... they've experienced a need to settle all pertinent documents and certificates, including a death certificate, at their local branch for closure", "Salman's EMI is outstanding due to his recent passing. Proper processes involving submission of his death certificate... are discussed"

9. **WNR - Wrong Number:**
   Wrong person contacted. Contact number doesn't belong to the customer OR Call receiver said he / she does not know the customer & didn't take loan for anyone from IDFC First. Also using this number since ( XX days)
   - **Indicators:** "मैं नहीं हूं", "wrong number", "नया sim", "नहीं जानते उनको".
   - **Pattern:** " नहीं अमित कुमार नहीं हूँ", "मैं राहुल बोल रहा हूं राहुल... नहीं भाई साहब मैं तो गया sim जी अभी दो महीने से लिए", "नहीं नहीं यह wrong number है... यह number मेरे हैं मैंने banking कोई नहीं लिए हैं"

10. **LMS - Left Message:**
    In the absence of the customer, someone else who is relative of the customer and who knows the customer has received the call. Message left with family member.
    - **Indicators:** "उनको बता दीजिए", "inform करवा दीजिए ki call आया था loan के regarding".
    - **Pattern:** "उनके पास जो फ़ोन है वहीं छोड़कर गए हैं। वो जैसे आते हैं आप उनको inform करवा दीजिए फिर कि IDFC First Bank की ओर से जो है ना call आया हुआ था", "आप उनकी wife को बोल दीजिए कि IDFC First Bank की तरफ़ से call आया था loan के regarding"

11. **LBR - Language Barrier:**
    Communication issues due to language preferences.
    - **Indicators:** "हिंदी नहीं आती", "कन्नड में callback", "बंगाली comfortable", regional language requests.
    - **Pattern:** "सिर आपकी due date थी... आपकी emi bounce हुई सर... कन्नड कन्नड", "कौन सी भाषा में comfortable हो... कौन सी language आती है... बंगाली बंगाली हां बंगाली", "हिंदी नहीं आती... बंगाली करो", "हिंदी नहीं... ओडिया में कॉल"

12. **CBK - Call Back explicitly requested by the customer:**
    Customer explicitly asks the agent to call back at a particular date / time. Customer can ask the agent to callback as he might be busy - maybe he is driving, OR facing network issue. If there was some EXPLICIT callback requested by the customer with timeline that is CBK - Call Back.
    - **Exception:** Call back due to language barrier falls under LBR not CBK.

13. **CHP - Call Hang Up:**
    Communication breakdown, customer hung up the call before identification, so very short transcript. Call went to voice mail.
    - **Indicators:** "hello hello" exchanges, non-responsiveness, line connectivity issues.
    - **Pattern:** The representative repeatedly asking if the customer can hear them. The call is filled with 'hello' exchanges.

14. **PPC - Probable Problematic Customer:**
    Rude/problematic behavior flagged.
    - **Indicators:** "rude behavior", "aggressive".

15. **OTH - Other:**
    Any case that doesn't clearly fit the above categories or has insufficient information for classification.

**Final Rule for evaluation:**

A. Read the call transcript carefully.
B. Starting from the top of the priority list check—one disposition at a time—whether that disposition accurately matches the primary purpose, outcome, or definitive customer stance of the call.
    - The **FIRST** match that can best describe the entire intent of the call wins.
    - If nothing matches, choose **OTH**.
    - **PRIORITY ORDER:** CPD → ONP → PTP → FPTP → DIR → RTP → LDD → CDE → WNR → LMS → LBR → CBK → CHP → PPC → OTH
C. Think through the match criteria step-by-step **in your head** (chain-of-thought).
D. Always cite explicit transcript lines that led to your answer.
E. Reflect on the answer that you gave.
