#### Process to be followed on SFDC:
- Step i. All the list of verifiers/validation level are mapped against each tagging category and subcategory
- Step ii. Upon selecting a category and subcategory, a validation screen will prompt with the level of validation required
- Step iii. BOC needs to select If a call is received from Registered Mobile Number or the PIN is verified
- Step iv. The list of verifiers will be available on the screen for the user to capture the verification sought from the customers to assure compliance (Refer below screenshot)
 **Note: In case customer calls and needs assistance on multiple accounts on the same call, CSE to follow the below (Applicable only to Medium & Low risk queries):**
- Step i. In case customer wants assistance on a family members A/c, in such a scenario CSE to take verification from the credit card holder basis Registered Mobile Number / Non Registered Mobile Number verification parameters and assist with the credit card related query.

- Step ii) Note: - CSE to mandatorily take verification from the respective credit card holder prior to sharing any credit card related details.
- Step iii) CSE should not tell the customer to call back from Registered Mobile Number for assistance, instead follow Non Registered Mobile Number verification grid and assist the customer with their query.
#### Important Verification Parameters:
##### For CC Inbound Team:
- i. If the customer does not respond correctly, two additional questions will be asked for verification (Not applicable to HIGH Risk queries/requests). After three questions, ask the customer to call or come Pin verified with the correct verification information
- ii. Credit Limit, Card unblocking, PIN Reset, and UCIC (Unique Customer Identification Code) should only be shared if the call comes from a registered mobile number and High risk verification needs to be taken
- iii. The amount of +/- Rs. .100 as deviation is allowed while taking transaction related verification for e.g. 1 transaction in last 60 days, Bill Amount Last Paid etc.
- iv.Mother's maiden name:
- If mother's name is Pooja Devi as per SFDC and customer says Pooja, probe for full name
- Similarly, if mother's name is Pooja as per SFDC and customer says Pooja Devi, we can consider it positive
##### For Email Team:
- i. If an email is received from a registered email ID, it is considered a verified email
- ii. If the email is from registered email ID and officer needs to make a call, then the call to be made on registered mobile number updated on the account and take customers full name before giving account related information
- iii. If the email is from unregistered email, then customer will get an auto response informing him to send an email from registered email ID. If there is a need to call on such case, then inbound verification grid will be followed
- iv. If callback has been done for any reason example follow up then call needs to be made only on registered mobile number, CSE need to confirm full name on the call before giving any account related information
##### For Outbound Team:
- i. Call need to made only on registered mobile number, CSE need to confirm full name on the call before giving any account related information
- ii. In case the call is made for High-Ris query/request then we should HIGH Risk verification process
- iii. If call is made on unregistered number, then Inbound verification process need to be followed. IE call received from Non Registered Mobile Number
- iv. Converting Transaction to EMI - Banker On Call will call on registered mobile number. We can give out transaction details which is eligible to get converted to EMI before verification however officer need to take High Risk verification (applicable as per registered and Non-registered number) before converting the transaction to EMI
- V. Credit Card Acquisition - Since this a STP process officer need to take any 1 additional verification from list of verifier updated in verification grid

## How to identify PIN Verified Customer?
- To identify whether the call is pin verified or not, please refer to the 'Authentication Flag' .on CISCO


| Category            | Value     | Meaning             |
|---------------------|-----------|---------------------|
| Authentication Flag | Y         | Pin - Verified      |
| Authentication Flag | N / Blank | Non - verified call |

## Double Verification should not be taken
**Process Update:** If the customer has authenticated themselves on the IVR with Registered Mobile Number + Pin Authentication, CSE should not take double verification from the customer and should proceed with assisting them on their query or request.
-The following flow should be adhered to while attending a customer's call:
- 1. i.Check the current authentication type on the Cisco Finesse screen under section 'AUTH_TYPE'
- 2. ii.If the customer is authenticated on IVR, no additional verification is needed during the call.
- 3. iii.If the query/request is of high criticality, only then additional verification is required as per the LOB specific verification grid.
## Exceptional Process
- i. For foreclosure of individual EMI or Balance conversion into EMI, request to be accepted via call/email only if customer is calling from Registered Mobile Number or email is received from Registered email ID. Verification is to be taken as per verification grid.
- ii. Also, probe customer to provide exact details about EMIs to be foreclosed and then take action and for future, suggest him to foreclose individual EMI/Balance Conversion using Optimus (Mobile application/Net-banking)
- iii. But if the customer is calling from non-registered mobile number and post denial is still insisting, request customer to call from registered mobile number for foreclosure.
